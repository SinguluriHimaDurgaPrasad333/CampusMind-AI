CATEGORY: Fee-related Policies
EFFECTIVE DATE: Academic Year 2025-2026

TITLE: SEMESTER FEE PAYMENT SCHEDULE, LATE CHARGES, AND WITHDRAWAL REFUND POLICIES

1. FEE PAYMENT TIMELINE AND MODES
1.1 All tuition, laboratory, and campus facility fees must be paid per the following semester schedules:
    - Fall Semester (Odd): Full fee payable on or before July 25.
    - Spring Semester (Even): Full fee payable on or before December 24.
1.2 Payment must be made through the official CampusMind ERP payment gateway using Net Banking, UPI, NEFT, or Credit/Debit cards. Cash payments are not accepted.

2. LATE PAYMENT PENALTIES
2.1 Delays in semester fee settlement incur the following daily penalties:
    - Days 1 to 10 past due date: Late fee of INR 100 per calendar day.
    - Days 11 to 20 past due date: Late fee of INR 250 per calendar day.
    - Beyond 20 days: Student portal registration is suspended, course LMS access is restricted, and attendance will not be recorded until the fine and fee are cleared.

3. ADMISSION WITHDRAWAL AND TUITION FEE REFUND SCHEDULE
3.1 In accordance with University Grants Commission (UGC) mandates, refunds on withdrawal of admission are governed by the following tiers:
    - Tier 1: Formal withdrawal request submitted 15 days or more before the formally notified commencement date of classes: 100% refund of tuition and academic fees (minus a maximum deduction of INR 1,000 as processing fee).
    - Tier 2: Withdrawal request submitted less than 15 days before the commencement date of classes: 80% refund of fees.
    - Tier 3: Withdrawal request submitted within 15 days after the commencement date of classes: 50% refund of fees.
    - Tier 4: Withdrawal request submitted beyond 30 days after the commencement date of classes: 0% refund of tuition and academic fees.
3.2 Institutional Caution Deposit is 100% refundable after deducting pending campus dues or library clearances.
3.3 Approved refund claims are credited to the student's registered bank account within 15 business days.
