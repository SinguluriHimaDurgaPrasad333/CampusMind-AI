CAMPUS UNIVERSITY SCHOLARSHIP FOUNDATION
OFFICE OF STUDENT FINANCIAL AID & DEAN OF STUDENT WELFARE
DOCUMENT CODE: SCHOL-POL-2025
CATEGORY: Scholarship Information
EFFECTIVE DATE: Academic Year 2025-2026

TITLE: SCHOLARSHIP OPPORTUNITIES, FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE, AND ELIGIBILITY GUIDELINES

1. MERIT SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM (ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE)
1.1 Top 5% students in each academic department who achieve a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 9.0 or higher are awarded the Merit Scholarship.
1.2 Benefit: 50% waiver on tuition fees for the succeeding academic year.
1.3 Selection is automated based on the annual performance ranking generated by the Controller of Examinations.

2. NEED-BASED FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE (MEANS SCHOLARSHIP)
2.1 Designed to support economically underprivileged students pursuing higher education.
2.2 Eligibility:
    (a) Gross annual income of both parents/guardians combined must be less than INR 3,50,000 per annum (income certificate issued by Revenue Authority required).
    (b) Student must have passed all registered courses with a minimum CGPA of 7.0.
2.3 Concession Tiers:
    - Family income below INR 1,50,000: 75% tuition fee waiver.
    - Family income between INR 1,50,000 and INR 3,50,000: 40% tuition fee waiver.

3. SPORTS AND CO-CURRICULAR DISTINCTION SCHOLARSHIP
3.1 Students who have represented the State or Country in recognized National Games, World University Games, or Khelo India are eligible for sports concessions:
    - International representation / Medal: 100% tuition and hostel fee waiver.
    - National medal winner: 50% tuition waiver.

4. SCHOLARSHIP RENEWAL CONDITIONS
4.1 Scholarships are renewed annually subject to satisfactory academic and behavioral standing:
    (a) Maintain minimum CGPA of 8.0 (for Merit) or 7.0 (for Need-Based).
    (b) No backlogs or uncleared courses at the end of the academic year.
    (c) Minimum 80% aggregate attendance across all registered courses.
    (d) Zero disciplinary sanctions from the Proctorial Board.

5. APPLICATION PROCESS AND DEADLINES
5.1 Application window opens on August 1 and closes on August 31 annually via the University Financial Aid portal.
5.2 Incomplete applications or forged income certificates result in lifelong forfeiture and disciplinary rustication.
