EFFECTIVE DATE: Academic Year 2025-2026

TITLE: REGULATIONS GOVERNING STUDENT ATTENDANCE AND LEAVE OF ABSENCE

1. MINIMUM ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT
1.1 Every registered student is expected to attend 100% of the lectures, tutorials, and practical laboratory sessions scheduled for each registered course.
1.2 To be eligible to appear for the End-Semester Examination in any course, a student must secure a minimum attendance of 75% in aggregate across all lectures, tutorials, and laboratories of that specific course.
1.3 Students with attendance below 75% in any course will receive an Attendance Shortage Notice and shall be officially debarred from appearing in the End-Semester Examination for that particular course.
1.4 Debarred students will receive a grade of 'SA' (Shortage of Attendance), which carries 0 grade points, and must re-register for the course in a subsequent semester or summer term.

2. MEDICAL CONDONATION AND EXCEPTIONAL CIRCUMSTANCES
2.1 In genuine medical emergencies or hospitalization, the Dean of Academic Affairs may grant a condonation of attendance up to a maximum of 10% (i.e., lowering the eligibility requirement to 65%).
2.2 Under no circumstances shall any student with less than 65% attendance be permitted to sit for the end-semester examinations, regardless of medical validity.
2.3 Procedure for Medical Condonation:
    (a) The student must submit a formal medical leave application along with discharge summaries, prescriptions, and medical certificates issued by a registered medical practitioner.
    (b) The medical documents must be endorsed and verified by the Chief Medical Officer (CMO) at the University Health Center within 5 working days of resuming classes.
    (c) Late submissions of medical certificates after the deadline will be rejected.

3. ON-DUTY (OD) LEAVE FOR CO-CURRICULAR AND EXTRA-CURRICULAR ACTIVITIES
3.1 Students representing the University in approved inter-collegiate sports tournaments, national academic conferences, tech symposiums, cultural fests, or official university delegations are eligible for On-Duty (OD) attendance credit.
3.2 A maximum of 10 working days per semester may be sanctioned as OD leave.
3.3 Prior approval must be obtained in writing from the respective Head of Department (HOD) and Dean of Student Affairs at least 3 working days before the scheduled event.
3.4 Proof of participation or certificates must be submitted within 3 days of returning to the campus.

4. MONITORING AND ATTENDANCE DISPUTES
4.1 Course instructors update daily attendance on the University Student Portal (CampusMind ERP) within 24 hours of each class.
4.2 Students are responsible for reviewing their recorded attendance weekly.
4.3 Any discrepancy in recorded attendance must be reported to the course instructor within 7 calendar days of the relevant lecture. No attendance corrections will be entertained beyond 7 days.
4.4 The Final Debarment List is published on the University notice board and student portal 5 working days prior to the commencement of end-semester examinations.
