TITLE: GENERAL CAMPUS SERVICES, CERTIFICATE ISSUANCE, AND STUDENT AMENITIES FAQ

1. OFFICIAL TRANSCRIPTS AND BONAFIDE CERTIFICATES
1.1 How do students request official academic transcripts?
    - Students can apply online through the CampusMind Student ERP portal under 'Academic Requests' or visit the Registrar Office (Window 3).
    - Standard processing turnaround time is 5 to 7 working days.
    - Express processing (turnaround within 48 hours) is available upon payment of an expedited fee of INR 1,000.
1.2 How do students obtain a Bonafide / Enrollment Certificate for bus pass, passport, or bank loan purposes?
    - Bonafide certificates are generated digitally with cryptographic QR verification on the ERP portal within 24 hours of submission free of charge.

2. CENTRAL UNIVERSITY LIBRARY RULES
2.1 Operational Hours:
    - Regular Days: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
    - Examination Months (November-December and April-May): 24/7 round-the-clock reading hall access.
2.2 Borrowing Privileges:
    - Undergraduate students: Up to 4 books for 14 days, with 1 online renewal permitted if no reservation exists.
    - Overdue fine: INR 5 per day per overdue volume.

3. CAMPUS HEALTH AND EMERGENCY SERVICES
3.1 The University Health Center operates 24/7 with round-the-clock doctors, nurses, and two on-campus ICU ambulances.
3.2 General Outpatient (OPD) clinic hours: 8:30 AM to 7:00 PM Monday through Saturday.
3.3 Medical consultations and standard emergency medicines are completely free for all enrolled university students.
3.4 Emergency Contact: Ext 108 or Dial +91-011-555-HEALTH from any campus landline.

4. LOST ID CARD REPLACEMENT
4.1 If a student loses their RFID Smart ID Card:
    - Step 1: Immediately file an online report on the portal to block card access for campus gates and library.
    - Step 2: Pay the replacement fee of INR 300 at the Accounts Counter.
    - Step 3: Collect the newly encoded replacement card from the Proctor Office within 48 hours.

5. CAMPUS WI-FI ACCESS AND IT HELPDESK
5.1 High-speed campus-wide Wi-Fi (SSID: 'CampusMind-Secure') is available across all academic blocks, hostels, and libraries.
5.2 Login Credentials: Username is your University Student Registration Number; initial password is sent to your registered student email.
5.3 IT Helpdesk is located in Computing Center Building, Room 104 (Email: ithelpdesk@campusmind.edu, Ext 142).
