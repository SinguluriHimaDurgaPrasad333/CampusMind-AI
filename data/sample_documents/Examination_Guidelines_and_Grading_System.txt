TITLE: EXAMINATION REGULATIONS, EVALUATION SCHEME, AND GRADING SYSTEM

1. GRADING SYSTEM AND CGPA CALCULATION
1.1 The University follows a 10-point Letter Grading Scale as prescribed by statutory regulatory bodies:
    - O (Outstanding): Grade Point 10.0 (Marks >= 90%)
    - A+ (Excellent): Grade Point 9.0 (Marks 80% - 89%)
    - A (Very Good): Grade Point 8.0 (Marks 70% - 79%)
    - B+ (Good): Grade Point 7.0 (Marks 60% - 69%)
    - B (Above Average): Grade Point 6.0 (Marks 50% - 59%)
    - C (Pass): Grade Point 5.0 (Marks 45% - 49%)
    - F (Fail): Grade Point 0.0 (Marks < 45%)
    - SA (Shortage of Attendance): Grade Point 0.0
    - AB (Absent): Grade Point 0.0

2. PASSING CRITERIA AND ASSESSMENT BREAKUP
2.1 Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) accounts for 40% of the total course marks (Assignments, Quizzes, Mid-Term Exams).
2.2 End-Semester Examination accounts for 60% of the total course marks.
2.3 To pass a course, a student must secure:
    (a) At least 40% marks in the End-Semester Examination, AND
    (b) At least 45% aggregate marks combining CIA and End-Semester Examination.

3. RE-EVALUATION AND SCRUTINY PROCESS
3.1 Students may apply for Re-Evaluation or Re-Totalling of End-Semester answer scripts within 10 calendar days of grade declaration on the student portal.
3.2 A non-refundable scrutiny fee of INR 500 per theory course must be deposited online.
3.3 If the difference in re-evaluated marks is 5% or higher, the updated score will be reflected in the final grade card.

4. SUPPLEMENTARY / REMEDIAL EXAMINATIONS
4.1 Supplementary examinations are conducted annually in July for students with 'F' grades.
4.2 A student is permitted to appear in a maximum of 3 supplementary examinations in one sitting.

5. UNFAIR MEANS AND CODE OF CONDUCT
5.1 Electronic gadgets including mobile phones, programmable calculators, and smartwatches are strictly forbidden in examination halls.
5.2 Possession of unauthorized notes, copying, or communication will result in immediate confiscation of the answer booklet and referral to the Unfair Means Committee (UMC).
5.3 Disciplinary penalty includes debarment from all remaining papers of that session and rustication for one subsequent academic semester.
