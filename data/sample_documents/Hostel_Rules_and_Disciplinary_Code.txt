TITLE: STUDENT RESIDENTIAL CODE, CURFEW, AND HOSTEL CODE OF CONDUCT

1. HOSTEL ENTRY TIMINGS AND CURFEW
1.1 All resident students must enter their respective hostel gates by the following curfew hours:
    - Weekdays (Sunday to Thursday): 10:00 PM sharp.
    - Weekends (Friday and Saturday): 10:30 PM sharp.
1.2 Biometric / RFID attendance check is recorded between 10:00 PM and 10:45 PM nightly.
1.3 Disciplinary Penalties for Late Entry without prior sanction:
    - 1st Infraction: Written warning and INR 200 administrative fine.
    - 2nd Infraction: INR 500 fine and formal notification sent to parents/guardians.
    - 3rd Infraction: Loss of hostel residency privileges and mandatory suspension.

2. NIGHT-OUT AND OVERNIGHT LEAVE PERMISSION
2.1 Students intending to stay overnight outside the hostel premises must apply for a Night-Out Pass via the CampusMind ERP at least 24 hours in advance.
2.2 The leave application requires digital verification and OTP confirmation from the registered parent or local guardian before Warden approval.
2.3 No student may leave the hostel without an approved digital exit pass verified by security at the campus main gate.

3. PROHIBITED APPLIANCES AND FIRE SAFETY
3.1 For electrical safety, high-wattage heating and cooking appliances are strictly prohibited inside resident rooms:
    - Prohibited: Electric immersion water heaters, induction cooktops, hot plates, room heaters, electric kettles, and toaster ovens.
3.2 Possession of prohibited appliances will attract a fine of INR 2000, confiscation of the equipment, and disciplinary notice.
3.3 Personal laptops, desktop computers, mobile chargers, study lamps, and hair dryers under 500W are permitted.

4. QUIET HOURS AND GUEST POLICIES
4.1 Quiet Hours must be maintained across all hostel wings between 11:00 PM and 6:00 AM daily. Playing loud music or shouting in corridors is strictly forbidden.
4.2 Day visitors (parents and verified guardians) are permitted only in the central Hostel Visitor Lounge between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM.
4.3 Overnight guests and members of the opposite sex are strictly prohibited from entering student residential corridors.
4.4 Possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco products, cigarettes, vaping devices, or narcotics is grounds for immediate expulsion from the University.
